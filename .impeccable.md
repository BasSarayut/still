## Design Context

### Users
People making personal iPhone Lock Screen wallpapers from their own photos and text. Editing on iPhone and desktop is equally important. Thai is the interface language; content supports Thai, English and Japanese.

### Brand Personality
Quiet, personal, considered. Working product name: still.

### Aesthetic Direction
User-approved references: spacious light desktop editor, portrait music-player wallpaper with square artwork, track metadata, playback ornaments, extracted color strip and optional credit. Muted blue-gray accent, subtle rules, generous whitespace. Apple-like system typography is explicitly preferred over decorative fonts.

### Design Principles
- The wallpaper is the focal point; controls are secondary.
- Fixed composition, personal content. No freeform positioning or filters.
- Match preview and PNG using a shared canvas renderer.
- Keep images, processing and the latest draft on-device.
- Show lock-screen guides separately from exported artwork.
